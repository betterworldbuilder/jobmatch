Delete your JobsMatchNow account

Applies to the JobsMatchNow website, Android app and iOS app (developer: JobsMatchNow).

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Sign in at jobsmatchnow.com/app or in the JobsMatchNow app.
  2. Open the menu and choose Settings & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, email support@jobsmatchnow.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my account. We confirm by email once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, photo, CV and work details, preferences)
  • Your roles (for hiring teams), likes, matches and messages
  • Your sign-in details

Some records may be kept for a limited time where the law requires it (for example payment records), as described in our Privacy Policy.
