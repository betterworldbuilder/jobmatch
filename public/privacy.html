5. Matching, AI features and automated assistance

People decide, not software. A conversation opens only when both the candidate and the hiring team choose each other. JobsMatchNow never rejects, hires, or filters out a person on its own.

Fit scores. The fit score shown on profiles and roles is calculated with a fixed set of rules written by our team (skills, salary range, location or remote preference, experience). It is not produced by an AI model. It is a recommendation, not a guarantee, and the reasons behind each score are shown so you can judge it yourself.

Where we use AI. We use AI only to help people save time:

  • Reading a résumé/CV you upload, to suggest entries for your profile. You review and edit the suggestions; nothing is saved to your profile until you continue.
  • Reading a job description a hiring team pastes or uploads, to suggest the role's fields. The hiring team reviews and edits them before saving.
  • Preparing interviews (candidates): suggested topics and practice questions for a role. We do not give hiring teams AI-generated material about a candidate.
  • Conversation starters after a mutual match, which you can edit before sending.

What AI never does here: it never scores, ranks, or recommends hiring or rejecting a person. Everything it produces is marked “AI-generated suggestions, please review”.

AI provider. To provide these features, the relevant text (the résumé text or job description; for interview practice, the role; for conversation starters after a mutual match, the role, and the candidate's name, job title and listed skills) may be sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (the Claude API), which processes it on our behalf to return the suggestions. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its AI models. We send only what the feature needs, never payment details or passwords. When an AI feature is not available, the app uses simple non-AI templates instead.

Your own AI chatbot (optional, your choice). The in-app chatbot runs on your own AI account: you connect your own Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT) key. Only when you ask it a question do we send that question and a short summary of your own JobsMatchNow data (never your contact details or CV file) to the provider you chose, under your account and that provider's terms; usage is billed to you by that provider. Your key is stored encrypted and never shown again in full. The chatbot is read-only: it cannot apply, message or change anything. You can disconnect it at any time.

We may also use automated checks to detect spam, suspicious activity, or inappropriate language.

We do not intentionally use physical appearance, ethnicity, religion, pregnancy, sexual orientation, health status, or other protected characteristics to calculate job suitability.
