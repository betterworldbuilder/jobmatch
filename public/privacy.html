JobsMatchNow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Replaces the version of November 23, 2026

Looking for a job, or for the right person to hire, means sharing personal things: your career, your salary expectations, where you live. We built JobsMatchNow so that you stay in control of that information. This policy explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how to use your rights. It applies to the JobsMatchNow website, web app, and Android and iOS apps.

Our privacy promises

  • We never sell your personal data and never share it for advertising. There are no ads in JobsMatchNow.
  • Your contact details stay hidden until a mutual match. Before that, the other side sees no email, phone or contact links, no exact salary expectation, and only an anonymized preview of your CV.
  • Your exact location is never shown. Other members only see an approximate distance.
  • People decide, not software. Nothing in the app hires, rejects or filters out a person on its own.
  • Your data is not sent to AI by default and is never used to train AI. The only AI is the optional chatbot you connect with your own account.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app. Your profile, CV, photos and messages are deleted at once.
  • Passwords are hashed, payment card numbers never reach us, and website statistics are kept without IP addresses.

5. Matching, AI and automated decisions

In short: scores follow fixed, visible rules; AI only suggests; people always decide.

Fit scores are calculated with fixed rules written by our team (skills, salary range, location or remote preference, experience), not by an AI model, and the reasons are shown next to each score. A boosted ("promoted") card moves up the deck but its score never changes.

Helper features save you time with suggestions you review before saving:

  • reading a CV you upload, to suggest profile entries;
  • reading a job description a hiring team pastes or uploads, to suggest the role's fields;
  • interview practice for candidates (topics and questions for a role);
  • conversation starters after a mutual match, which you can edit before sending.

These features run on our own built-in rules on our servers. They do not send your data to any AI provider. If we ever connect them to an AI provider, we will update this policy and tell you before it applies. Nothing in JobsMatchNow scores, ranks, or recommends hiring or rejecting a person with AI, and we never give hiring teams AI-generated material about a candidate.

Your own chatbot runs on your own Anthropic or OpenAI account. Only when you ask a question do we send it, with a short summary of your own data (never your contact details or CV file), to the provider you chose. It is read-only and you can disconnect it at any time.

No solely automated decisions. A conversation opens only when people on both sides choose it. We may use automated checks to detect spam or suspicious activity; any suspension is decided by a person.

12. Your rights where you live

In short: the rights above apply everywhere; here is what your local law adds.

European Union, EEA and Switzerland

The GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection give you the rights in section 11, on the legal grounds in section 4. JobsMatchNow is based in France, so our lead authority is the CNIL (cnil.fr); you can also complain to the authority in the country where you live or work.

United Kingdom

The UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 give you the same rights. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

United States

If you live in California, the CCPA (as amended by the CPRA) gives you the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising. We use sensitive information (such as login details) only to provide the Service. You may use an authorized agent. Residents of other states with privacy laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and Oregon) have similar rights, including appealing our answer by replying to it.

Vietnam

Under Vietnam's personal data protection rules (the Law on Personal Data Protection and Decree 13/2023/ND-CP), we process your data with your consent or as the law permits. You have the right to be informed, to give and withdraw consent, to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict and object, to receive your data, and to complain. Your data is stored outside Vietnam (section 8); by creating an account you are informed of this transfer.

Singapore

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012, you may ask to access or correct your data and withdraw consent at any time (some features may then stop working). You can complain to the PDPC (pdpc.gov.sg).

Thailand

Under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), you have the rights to be informed, access, portability, objection, deletion or anonymization, restriction, correction and withdrawal of consent, and you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee.

Malaysia

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended in 2024), you may access and correct your data, withdraw consent, limit processing likely to cause damage or distress, prevent direct marketing, and ask for your data to be sent to another organization. You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

In short: only what the app needs to work. No advertising cookies.

We use one necessary cookie to keep you signed in securely, and your browser's storage for things like your current session, settings you chose and the website statistics session described in section 3. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies and show no ads. If we ever add optional analytics or marketing tools that need consent, we will ask you first.

14. Security

In short: we protect your data, and ask you to protect your login.

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), password hashing, encrypted storage of AI keys, access controls with admin sign-in, audit logs of admin actions, rate limits against abuse, and backups. Only authorized people at JobsMatchNow can access member data, and private messages are opened only to investigate a report, prevent fraud, resolve a support request you made, or comply with the law. No system is perfectly secure: use a strong password and tell us at once if you suspect someone accessed your account. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

15. Recruitment safety

In short: a real employer never asks you for money.

Report any fake vacancy, request for an application fee, deposit, equipment purchase, gift card or cryptocurrency, identity theft, harassment, discrimination or misuse of your data, using the Report option in the app or by writing to support@jobsmatchnow.com. Links to employer websites, video-call or calendar services are governed by those services' own policies.

16. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the product, our providers or the law change. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change matters to you, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
