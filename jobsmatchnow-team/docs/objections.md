# Objections & answers (rewritten 2026-10-25 for the US market)

Rules for answers: honest, no invented numbers. Any metric must come from `docs/metrics.md` (currently all [NEEDS DATA], so never cite traction, candidate counts, matches or hires). The mutual-match features are on a branch and go live soon: speak of them as "shipping soon" or "what you will see in the demo" until the founder confirms they are live. Do not make legal, tax or compliance claims; we do not provide payroll, EOR or legal advice. "Worked?" is filled in by the founder after real calls.

## Sales objections (US hiring teams)

| Objection | From | Best answer | Worked? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Time zones: Vietnam/SEA is 12-15 hours off US time." | sales | Fair, and it is the first thing to settle. Some roles work well with a few hours of overlap plus async work, others do not. We settle the overlap a role needs at the start, before anyone spends time on interviews. If the role needs full US business hours, it is probably not a fit and we will say so. (Do not claim overlap matching or overlap filtering in the product: timezone-overlap matching is NOT STARTED in `docs/exit-readiness.md`. Reviewer, 2026-10-25.) | |
| "How do we know the engineers are any good? What is the vetting?" | sales | Be precise: what you see on a match is the explainable fit, the reasons this person matches your role, and the salary range. You judge quality in your own interview; nothing auto-hires or auto-rejects. Do not describe our vetting process, tests or screening until the founder confirms exactly what exists today. Offer the free pilot so you can test candidate quality on one role at no cost. | |
| "Contractor or employer of record? Payroll, taxes, compliance?" | sales | We are a matching app, not a payroll provider, EOR or legal adviser. How you engage someone abroad (contractor or through an EOR) is your decision with your own counsel or provider, and many teams already use a payroll vendor for that. We stay out of that layer and help you find the person first. If you want a vendor recommendation, say so and we can talk about it. | |
| "IP protection: who owns the code if the engineer is abroad?" | sales | That is set by your contract, not by us. Teams normally use a written agreement with IP assignment and confidentiality, reviewed by their own lawyer. We do not provide legal terms and do not claim anything about enforceability in any country. If it helps, we can note that concern on the role so candidates know the setup before they match. | |
| "Why not Toptal?" | sales | Toptal is a managed vetted network with matching done for you, typically priced accordingly. We are a different model: you see and choose candidates directly, mutual opt-in, salary up front. If you want a done-for-you vendor, Toptal may fit. We suit teams who want direct control and lower overhead. (Do not quote competitor prices; check `docs/competitors.md` first.) | |
| "Why not Upwork?" | sales | Upwork is a large open marketplace, strong for short freelance work and volume. We focus on engineers looking for ongoing remote roles with a US team, and a conversation only opens when both sides say yes, so no applicant pile. Use both if you like; test us on one role. | |
| "Why not Deel (or other EOR)?" | sales | Deel handles contracts, payroll and compliance after you have found someone. We help you find the person. They are complementary: match here, hire through Deel or your existing provider. | |
| "Why not Turing?" | sales | Turing offers vetted engineers and managed placement. We are a lighter direct-match tool where you choose and the candidate chooses back. If you want a fully managed service, Turing is a reasonable option. If you want to see candidates yourself, this is the fit. (Check `docs/competitors.md` before comparing details.) | |
| "Why not Arc?" | sales | Arc is a remote job and talent marketplace. We differ on mutual opt-in, explainable fit and salary up front, and we are new. Use the pilot to compare on one role. Do not claim feature superiority beyond what the demo shows. | |
| "We already post on LinkedIn / YC Work at a Startup / Wellfound." | sales | Keep them. Those are where people apply; we are where both sides have already said yes. Treat us as an extra channel for the same roles, tested on a free pilot. | |
| "How are your engineers vetted?" | sales | Be precise about what exists. We are defining a badge for engineers who pass our checks, and the badge definition is pending the founder, so do not describe a finished process or name the badge as live. What a passing engineer is assessed on: technical depth, English, async work habits, USD pay expectation and overlap hours. You still judge quality yourself in your own interview, and the pilot lets you test it on one role at no cost. Never give a count of vetted engineers or a pass rate until they are in `docs/metrics.md`. Criteria from Mark, 2026-10-27 (`outbox/2026-10-27_marketer_vetting-pipeline.md`). | |
| "Swiping feels unserious for hiring." | sales | The swipe is just the quick first yes or no. Before choosing you see the explainable fit and the salary range, so the decision is informed. A conversation opens only after both sides say yes, which is more respectful than cold messages. We can show it on a role of yours in the demo. | |
| "Will there be any candidates on a new app?" | sales | Honest answer: we are new, and I will not claim a candidate count I cannot back up. Today I can show the demo matching live. The pilot is free precisely because we are building the Vietnam/SEA engineer side with design partners. Ask for the numbers on the call and we will share only what is in our metrics file. | |
| "Salary up front: we lose negotiating room." | sales | You set the range, and a wide band is enough. It filters out candidates who would reject the offer later and saves interview time on both sides. Start wide and tighten after you see who matches. | |
| "What does it cost? No budget for another tool." | sales | The pilot is free for 3 months on one role. After that, pricing is a simple fee per hire, so you pay only when you hire someone (`docs/decisions.md` 2026-10-25). Do not quote an amount until the founder sets it. No charge during the pilot. | |
| "Is candidate and company data private? Security?" | sales | Candidates control what they share and a chat opens only after a mutual match. Do not claim certifications (SOC 2, GDPR, encryption specifics) until the builder and founder confirm in writing. Offer to share our data handling in plain words on request. | |
| "How do we know match quality is good? Is the AI biased?" | sales | We show the reasons for each fit so your team can judge and disagree. You make every decision; nothing auto-rejects or auto-hires. Do not claim bias testing or accuracy figures unless the builder provides evidence. | |
| "We have no time to set up." | sales | Setup is meant to be small: describe the role, salary range and must-haves in a 15-minute demo. No migration from your current tools. (Confirm actual setup time with the builder before promising.) | |
| "Communication / English level worries us." | sales | Reasonable. You can talk to the candidate before any commitment, and a conversation opens only on mutual match. Do not claim English proficiency of candidates in general; check what profile fields exist first. | |
| "We only hire US-based / US-citizen engineers." | sales | Then this is not a fit today; thanks for being clear. If that changes for any role, we can revisit. (Qualify out; do not push.) | |
