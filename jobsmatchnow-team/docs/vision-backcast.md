## The story, told by each team member

### Founder (CEO + community lead): "I sold by hand, vetted engineers in person, and never faked a number."
- **Strategy:** one milestone at a time; approve everything outbound; say no to anything off the path.
  Also the face of the Vietnam/SEA engineer community: live technical interviews, dev meetups, referrals
  (decision 2026-10-27). Agents prepared every interview and event; the founder showed up.
- **Steps:** pivoted to US buyers (real, 2026-10-25) → C-corp in month 1 → onboarded every pilot personally →
  weekly metrics from week 1 → ran both acquirer conversations myself.
- **Success:** 3 years of steady weekly metrics. Diligence trusted the numbers.
- **Failure:** the Vietnam-local launch found no paying buyers. Lesson: the buyer is in the US, the talent is in VN/SEA.

### Bui (builder): "I built what LinkedIn could plug in on day one."
- **Strategy:** differentiators in production first, then only what pilots or the acquirer asked for:
  LinkedIn sign-in, Greenhouse, clean event data, SOC2.
- **Success:** privacy-first analytics passed diligence without rework.
- **Failure:** the ATS integration slipped a quarter behind features nobody asked for. Lesson: build nothing without a requester.

### Rev (reviewer): "Nothing we said had to be taken back."
- **Strategy:** every claim traced to `docs/metrics.md` or a public link; every change reviewed before staging.
- **Success:** zero retractions; "every claim has a source" took one day in diligence.
- **Failure:** nearly let a deck say "hundreds of teams" when it was 40. Lesson: review decks before rehearsal.

### Mark (marketer): "Engineers and US founders both believed in mutual match."
- **Strategy:** one message: *"Let the perfect match chase you."* Dev communities + referrals for candidates;
  LinkedIn posts on real hiring news for US founders.
- **Success:** posting about hiring *on LinkedIn* got LinkedIn's product people to notice us before corp dev did.
- **Failure:** the first candidate push brought sign-ups, not vetted engineers. Lesson: 1,000 vetted beats 10,000 sign-ups.

### Selly (seller): "I found the 10 teams that proved it, then the rest."
- **Strategy:** tight niche (remote-first US dev-tools/infra startups that hire globally), free 3-month pilot,
  per-hire fee after.
- **Success:** per-hire pricing kept pilot-to-paid conversion high.
- **Failure:** two early pilots went quiet. Lesson: no first match in 7 days = dead pilot; onboard personally.

### Rizer (fundraiser): "I put a second bidder in the room."
- **Strategy:** US money only; reach LinkedIn through partnership and visible growth, not a cold pitch;
  keep Recruit/Indeed, ZipRecruiter and the ATS companies warm.
- **Success:** Recruit's interest is what moved LinkedIn to $100M.
- **Failure:** drafted acquirer emails in week 1 (real drafts, 2026-10-25). Lesson: hold them until milestone 7.
