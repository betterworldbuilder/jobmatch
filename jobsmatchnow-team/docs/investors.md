## What every fund here will ask (from the comparables)
- The comparables that got funded show **revenue or a large user base**. Dex had more than 15,000 engineers, more than 50 employers and about $1.8M run rate at seed. HiringCafe had more than 2M MAU at pre-seed. Saile had more than 5,000 physicians. Our numbers are all **[NEEDS DATA]** (`docs/metrics.md`). The accelerators (speedrun, YC) are the only realistic targets before pilots produce data.
- Dex charges employers 20-30% of salary. That gives a public reference point for the per-hire fee decision (P4). It is a reference only, not our price.
