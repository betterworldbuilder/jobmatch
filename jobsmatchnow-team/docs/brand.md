# Brand

- Voice: warm, confident, playful about the "match" idea, never cheesy or dating-app sleazy in B2B copy.
- Colors: pink #fd267a → orange gradient, navy text.
- Say: "mutual match", "explainable fit", "salary up front", "the match chases you".
- Avoid: "revolutionary", "#1", "guaranteed hire", unexplained "AI-powered".
- Do/don't (2026-10-27, from Rev and the M1 meeting):
  - Don't write "vetted" or "pre-vetted" in any public copy until the founder defines it (P2 in `docs/decisions.md`, aid in `outbox/2026-10-27_marketer_vetted-definition.md`).
  - Do write "the demo shows ..." for any feature not yet in production (mutual match, explainable fit, salary up front, badges) until Bui confirms the branch is live.

## Positioning (marketer, 2026-10-25)

Launch market: software engineers and the tech startups (10-200 people) hiring them in Ho Chi Minh City. Personas refer to `docs/icp.md`. Every proof point below is a shipped product fact (on a branch, live soon). No traction numbers; any metric must come from `docs/metrics.md`.

### For candidates (C1 mid-level dev, C2 senior passive)
**Line:** "Stop applying into the void. Teams that chose you, salary shown first."

**Supporting statement:** You see the pay before you spend a minute, and you see why a team fits you. A conversation opens only when the team picks you too, so there are no cold DMs and no CV left unanswered in a pile. You can try matching in the demo without signing up.

**Pains it answers (icp.md):** C1 "applying into the void", "salary hidden until late", "unclear whether the stack/team will suit them"; C2 "dozens of recruiter DMs with no salary".

**Proof points it rests on:**
- Mutual match before any chat opens.
- Salary required on every role.
- Explainable fit (why this team matches you).
- Private distance range ("coffee in your city", commute-aware).
- Demo matching without signup; installable web app.

**Do not claim:** that a team will reply, that you will get hired, that your search is hidden from your current employer (not confirmed), or any candidate/team counts.

### For hiring teams (B1 founder/CTO, B2 Head of Talent, B3 hiring manager as champion)
**Line:** "Talk only to engineers who already chose your role."

**Supporting statement:** Post a real role in one short form, with the salary range up front, and see each candidate's explainable fit before you decide. A chat opens only after both sides say yes, so you spend your time on conversations, not on sifting CVs. Design partners get 3 months free in exchange for real roles, feedback and a testimonial.

**Pains it answers (icp.md):** B1 "inbound CVs are mostly irrelevant", "hiring takes their own coding/product time", "candidates ghost after interview"; B2 "high CV volume with low fit", "salary mismatch discovered late", "paying several job boards with unclear ROI"; B3 "unqualified interviews", "fits explained on one screen".

**Proof points it rests on:**
- Mutual match before any chat opens (intent is confirmed on both sides).
- Explainable fit: reasons shown, your team makes the call, nothing auto-rejects or auto-hires.
- Salary required on every role (filters out offer-stage surprises).
- Post a role in one short form (do not quote minutes until builder confirms: [NEEDS DATA]).
- Free 3-month design-partner offer; use alongside LinkedIn and job boards, not instead of them.

**Do not claim:** more or better hires, a candidate count, time saved in numbers, bias testing, accuracy, privacy-law compliance (see `docs/objections.md` rows 3, 5, 7, 11).

### Competitive framing (hypothesis, see `docs/competitors.md`)
We are an extra channel, not a replacement for LinkedIn or job boards: they bring applicants, we bring a pre-agreed yes from both sides.
