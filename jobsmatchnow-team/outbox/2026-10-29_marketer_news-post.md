## Post (about 150 words)

Recruiting tools are starting to remember what the recruiter likes.

That is useful. The tool learns your taste, so the shortlist gets closer to what you want.

But a hiring decision has two sides. A tool that learns the recruiter's preferences still says nothing about what the candidate wants, or whether they would say yes.

So I am building the other half: a match only becomes a conversation when both sides choose it. You see why a person fits your role. They see the pay range before they spend time on you.

My honest view: memory on one side makes the pile better sorted. It does not make the pile smaller.

If you hire engineers remotely: what is one thing you wish you knew about a candidate before the first call? Reply, I read every one.
