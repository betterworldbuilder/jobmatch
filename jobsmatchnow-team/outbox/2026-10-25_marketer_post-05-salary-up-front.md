"If we post the salary up front, don't we lose negotiating room?"

It's a fair question. Here's how I think about it.

You set the range, and a wide band is fine. What you lose is the candidate who'd turn down the offer at the very end, after three interviews. That's the most expensive kind of "no" in hiring.

Every role on JobsMatchNow has a salary range, and engineers see it before they match. Start wide, then tighten once you see who's matching.

Do you post ranges today? What changed your mind either way?
