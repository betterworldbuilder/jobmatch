Engineers in Vietnam and Southeast Asia: here's what US startups are likely to ask first about remote hires.

1. How many hours can you overlap with US time?
2. How do you work async: written updates, docs, pull requests?
3. How comfortable are you in English on calls and in writing?
4. Have you worked with a remote or international team before?

None of these is about where you live. They're about how you work.

If you're aiming for US remote roles, say these things clearly on your profile. Teams decide faster when they don't have to guess.
