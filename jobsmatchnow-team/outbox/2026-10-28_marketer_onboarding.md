## 1. Hiring-team LinkedIn post (founder's personal profile)

Persona: B1 founder/CTO. Metric to move: clicks on "Test demo matching" and replies/DMs from hiring leads.

You post a role. Hundreds of CVs arrive. Most are not a fit, and you read them anyway.

I think hiring is backwards. So we are building JobsMatchNow around one rule: a conversation opens only when BOTH sides say yes.

Here is what the demo shows:

- A mutual match first. No cold applications, no cold DMs.
- Explainable fit. You see why a candidate matches your role, and your team makes the call. Nothing is auto-rejected.
- Salary up front. Every role carries a pay range, so offer-stage surprises show up on day one.

It is meant to sit next to LinkedIn and your job boards, not replace them. They bring applicants. We aim to bring people who already chose your role.

We are early, and I would rather you judge it yourself than take my word.

Try the demo matching, no signup: https://jobsmatchnow.com/app/

If you hire remote engineers, I am also offering a free 3-month pilot. Send me a DM.
