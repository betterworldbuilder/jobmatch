## 4-week first plan (fits a limited founder budget: aim for about 2 hours a week, tell me if it is more or less)

Principle: give before you ask. Two goals only: (1) learn what engineers want from US remote work (feeds docs/icp.md), (2) get interviews booked with strong engineers. Do not push the app in week 1.

### Week 1: look and listen (about 1.5 hours)
- Join 3 groups: #6 VBI Dev & Tech Forum (reach), #1 GDG Cloud Hanoi (cloud/backend depth), #7 ITLC (seniors). Check each group's last post date first and read the rules.
- Do not post. Read the last 20 posts in each and note: what do people ask about, do they discuss US remote jobs, USD pay, contracting. Mark logs notes in docs/icp.md Learnings.
- Mark prepares for the founder: a one-paragraph founder intro for each group (below).

### Week 2: introduce yourself, ask a question (about 2 hours)
- Post one intro in the 2 groups that allow it. Draft (adapt, Vietnamese translation by the founder or a native reviewer):
  "Hi everyone, I am [NAME], building JobsMatchNow. The demo shows teams and engineers both opting in before any conversation, with pay shown up front. I am talking with Vietnamese engineers who want remote work for international teams. What is the hardest part of finding those roles: salary clarity, contracting, English interviews, or trust in the company? Happy to share what I learn back here."
- Reply to every comment for 3 days. Offer a 15-minute chat to anyone who answers in detail (these are your first interview leads).
- Join #4 Saigon.rb and #9 Meetup HCMC listings; RSVP to one event for week 4.
- Do not post a signup link yet. Share a link only if someone asks.

### Week 3: give something useful (about 2 hours)
- Share one practical post in the groups that allowed week 2 (Mark drafts, founder approves): for example "5 questions US startups ask in remote engineer interviews" or "What contracting for a US startup usually involves [NEEDS DATA: verify with a lawyer before giving guidance]". Keep it free of unsupported claims.
- Soft CTA, one line: "If you want to try matching with teams that show pay up front, test the demo here: [LINK]" Demo matching needs no signup, per CLAUDE.md. Only include after the founder confirms the demo is live.
- Book 3 to 5 interviews (stage 4 in outbox/2026-10-27_marketer_vetting-pipeline.md) from people who engaged. Run your first live interviews to calibrate the scorecard.

### Week 4: show up in person (about 3 hours, includes travel)
- Attend one meetup. Pick from the live calendars (#9, #10, or the GDG Hanoi/HCMC event pages) one with an engineering topic in the city you are in. DevFest Hanoi (#2) is the ideal target if the 2026 date is in range [NEEDS DATA: date unverified].
- What to say in person (30 seconds): "I run an engineer community for remote roles with international teams. Not a recruiter. I am interviewing engineers about what they want. Can I ask you 3 questions?" Ask: what stops you applying to US startups; what pay would make you switch; what would make you trust a team. Take notes only with permission.
- Collect: names/handles only from people who say yes to a follow-up. Never record personal emails/phones in docs (CLAUDE.md feedback rule). Follow up within 48 hours with a 3-line message Mark drafts.
- End of week 4: Mark writes a one-page learnings entry (what worked, what did not) into docs/icp.md Learnings.

## Success measures for the 4 weeks (all baselines [NEEDS DATA])
Candidate sign-ups from community links; interviews held; interviews passing; replies per post; top 3 pain points quoted back. Stop date: end of week 4 (2026-11-24). If a group produces nothing after 2 posts, drop it.

## What agents prepare vs what only the founder can do
Agents: intros, post drafts, Vietnamese-ready outlines, interview prep sheets, notes templates, learnings log. Founder: joining, posting from a personal account, replying, interviewing, showing up in person.
