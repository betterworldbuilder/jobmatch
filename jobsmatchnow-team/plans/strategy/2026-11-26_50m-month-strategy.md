# Strategy: $50M per month, then the option to sell (founder, 2026-11-26)

Status: adopted by the founder on 2026-11-26. It sits on top of `Jobmatchplaybook.md` (stages and gates) and
reframes the end goal. All figures are DRAFT scenarios, never facts in outbound text (team rule 3).

## The goal
Build JobsMatchNow to **$50 million in monthly revenue** ($600M a year), then create the **option** to sell it to
LinkedIn or another strategic buyer. There is no guaranteed buyer or price; at that size the founder decides whether
to sell, keep growing, or do something else.

## Paying customers needed for $50M/month (simple scenarios)
| Average revenue per paying customer | Paying customers needed |
|---|---|
| $50/month | 1,000,000 |
| $100/month | 500,000 |
| $500/month | 100,000 |
| $1,000/month | 50,000 |
| $5,000/month | 10,000 |

Candidates stay free; recruiters pay. Real revenue depends on plan mix, upgrades and discounts.

## A possible long-term mix (illustrative, not a forecast or a price list)
| Segment | Paying customers | Average monthly revenue | Monthly total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small recruiters and businesses | 300,000 | $50 | $15M |
| Growing recruiting teams | 100,000 | $150 | $15M |
| Larger agencies and employers | 20,000 | $1,000 | $20M |
| **Total** | **420,000** | — | **$50M** |

Validate demand before designing an enterprise offer.

## Working backward from $50M/month
1. Enough paying customers at a suitable average price.
2. Recruiters who stay and pay repeatedly because the product helps them hire.
3. Relevant candidate activity in the markets and job categories those recruiters need.
4. A repeatable acquisition system for recruiters and candidates.
5. A product that delivers measurable outcomes: relevant matches, conversations, interviews and hires.
6. One successful first market that proves the process can be repeated.

It starts with **one successful introduction**, then a repeatable workflow, not with building every feature.

## Why would a recruiter pay every month?
Proposition to test: **"JobsMatchNow helps me find relevant, interested candidates and start useful conversations
with less effort."**

Funnel: job published → relevant candidates discover it → candidates express interest → recruiter responds →
conversation → interview → hire.

- Relevant conversations but no payment → investigate pricing and value.
- Candidates like jobs but recruiters don't respond → fix recruiter participation before scaling candidates.

## Stages
| Stage | Objective | Evidence before moving on |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prove the connection | Make JobFinder useful in one job category and market | Real candidate–recruiter conversations and feedback |
| 2. Prove willingness to pay | Recruiters use JobsMatchNow for real roles | Paid customers or documented paid commitments |
| 3. Prove repeatability | Recruiters return and use it again | Repeat postings, recurring payments, hires |
| 4. Expand | More categories and locations | New markets produce outcomes without the founder's personal push |
| 5. Scale revenue | More customers, more revenue each | Sustainable acquisition cost, retention, service cost |
| 6. Explore an exit | Talk to strategic buyers | Credible financials, product evidence, a reason a buyer values us |

## What we do now (Stage 1: prove the connection)
Market choice (founder, 2026-11-26): **any company**, both US startups hiring remote Vietnam engineers **and**
Ho Chi Minh City local companies. Category: **AI and Data**. Founder time: **1 hour per day**.

1. Select 20–30 current AI and Data jobs in one market.
2. Recruit 5 employers or recruiters willing to receive introductions.
3. Bring relevant candidates to those jobs through the founder's JobsMatchNow LinkedIn posts.
4. Measure: Likes → introductions → recruiter replies → conversations → interviews.
5. Ask participating recruiters to try a specific paid offer after they see value.

**The question to answer first:** can JobsMatchNow repeatedly create recruiter–candidate conversations that
recruiters value enough to pay for? If yes, work backward from the measured conversion and retention rates to the
number of jobs, candidates, recruiters and channels needed for $50M/month.
